Our Story

Where Every Cake
Tells a Story

"A cake is not just dessert — it is the centrepiece of your most cherished moments."

At Chathu Cake, we believe that every celebration deserves something truly extraordinary. Founded in the heart of Sri Lanka, our artisan bakery combines time-honoured techniques with modern artistry to create cakes that are as beautiful as they are delicious.

Each creation is handcrafted using the finest local ingredients — fresh butter, single-origin chocolate, and seasonal blooms. From intimate birthday gatherings to grand wedding receptions, we pour our hearts into every tier.
